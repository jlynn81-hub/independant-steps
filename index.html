Make the Bed

  1. Pull up the sheets.
  2. Place pillows at the top.
  3. Smooth the blanket.

Do the Laundry

  1. Separate colors from whites.
  2. Load the washer.
  3. Add detergent.
  4. Press power and pick setting.
  5. Start the wash.

Feed the Pets

  1. Measure the correct amount of pet food.
  2. Serve the food at the same times every day.
  3. Wash the food and water bowls daily.
